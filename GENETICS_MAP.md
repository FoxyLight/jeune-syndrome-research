## Checkpoint contract

- Question: Which genes and mechanisms are supported in the Jeune / asphyxiating thoracic dystrophy (ATD) and relevant short-rib thoracic dysplasia (SRTD) spectrum, and what phenotype or variant conclusions are justified?
- Starting authority: CP1 PASS / CLOSED. Approved CP1 baseline 19ae0eb361cc79463acf0cc1f3923ad548febd7f; closure record and starting HEAD 58fd7cc5da7d103cb0fbf8462ed1894e4b9bf507. Master policy and specification remain unchanged. Explicit user instruction: "CP2 authorized."
- Scope: association, molecular role, source-specific thoracic, renal, hepatic, retinal and skeletal findings, survival observations, important illustrative variants, association strength, and uncertainty. Include classification-listed SRTD genes and bounded newer or related reports without merging their populations.
- Exclusions: patient diagnosis, individual pathogenicity reassessment, treatment or surveillance recommendations, comprehensive adult-outcome review, frontier therapeutics, CP3 work, and commit/push.
- Required evidence: classification authority, current nomenclature, primary human studies for consequential phenotype claims, contradictory evidence, current curated association information, and explicit source/version/access limits.
- Verification: trace claims to source records; distinguish people, families, screening populations and experimental models; preserve possible overlap; verify variant transcript and dated database assertions where used; leave unsupported fields unknown.
- Decision criterion: a bounded, reviewable map with G1-G6, explicit evidence grades, and no deterministic prediction or pooled gene-specific prognosis.
- Stopping rule: stop when the consequential distinctions and contradictions are resolved within available sources. Missing phenotype detail is a valid result, not permission to fill gaps by inference.
- Approval boundary: stop before authoritative adoption, CP2 closure, Git integration or CP3. Research authorization does not authorize those steps.

## Reading the map

Autosomal recessive means disease generally requires relevant variants affecting both gene copies. Biallelic means both copies are affected; compound heterozygous means the two variants differ. A gene association does not establish every variant in that gene as pathogenic.

Intraflagellar transport (IFT) moves material within cilia. IFT-A and IFT-B are transport complexes; dynein-2 is the return-transport motor. Hedgehog and Wnt are cell signaling pathways involved in skeletal development. A protein's experimental role does not quantify human organ risk. [E16, E18, E32]

The 2023 classification has 21 gene-associated SRTD entries at NOS 10-0130 through 10-0330. This is a classification-defined starting set, not 21 equally validated prognosis models. IFT74 and FGF4 are additional, separately assessed reports. Related SRPS, cranioectodermal, Joubert and other diagnoses retain their source labels. Gene counts are not asserted to be exhaustive as of 2026. [E02, E34, E35]

Unknown below means not established for that gene and phenotype in this intake. It does not mean absent, normal or zero risk. For every record, lifetime organ risks and population survival rates are unknown. Age/follow-up detail absent from an abstract is explicitly not verified. Each gene is recessive in the cited SRTD classification; other phenotypes or inheritance models are not automatically interchangeable.

## Candidate conclusions for approval

- G1. Established classification and nomenclature; variable association confidence. The map retains the 21 classification-listed genes, current aliases, and separate newer/related reports. Classification, PanelApp color and formal gene-disease validity grades are different evidence systems. [E02, E14, E15, E28]
- G2. Established broad mechanism; gene-specific limits. Most mapped genes participate in cilia assembly, transport or signaling. GRK2 provides a signaling example with preserved cilia morphology. FGF4 is a newer thoracic dystrophy association whose specific human mechanism and reported variant effects remain unconfirmed. [E16, E18, E32, E35]
- G3. Probable qualitative genotype-phenotype patterns. IFT140, IFT172 and WDR19 reports support renal or retinal disease alongside skeletal disease; CFAP410 also has retinal-predominant presentations. These selected studies do not establish relative frequencies or rule out organs in other genotypes. [E07, E18, E19, E29]
- G4. Probable heterogeneity, unknown individual prediction. Severity varies within genes and families. DYNC2H1 and DYNLT2B observations, and GRK2 siblings with different courses, prevent a deterministic gene-to-survival rule. Allele-specific hypotheses require validation. [E06, E24, E25, E32]
- G5. Established interpretation distinction. Association, a reported variant, functional evidence, a submitter's classification and expert-reviewed pathogenicity are distinct. The examples below preserve transcripts, dates, review strength and uncertainty. This map does not override clinical laboratory interpretation. [E28, E34, E35]
- G6. Unknown quantitative prognosis and incomplete coverage. No pooled diagnostic yield, gene-specific survival rate, penetrance estimate or lifetime organ-risk estimate is adopted. FGF4 replication and functional evidence, rare-gene phenotypes, cohort overlap and inaccessible full texts remain material gaps. [E05-E07, E15, E24, E33-E35]

## Dynein-2 genes

### DYNC2H1

Association: classification-recognized Jeune/SRTD and SRPS; established association, probable qualitative phenotype. Role: motor heavy chain. Thorax/skeleton: variable short ribs and narrow chest, including sibling differences. Kidney/retina: abnormalities occur despite a predominantly skeletal presentation in one series. Liver: findings reported. Survival: older survivors and severe early presentations; no gene-specific rate. Population: E06 screened 71 people/57 families; findings in 29 people/19 families; fetal through adult ages, variable follow-up. Primary references: E06; E09 provides a recent selected series. Limits: ascertainment, uncertain variants in some recent cases, and possible overlap.

### DYNC2LI1

Association: classification-recognized SRTD; probable source-specific phenotype. Role: motor light intermediate chain. Thorax/skeleton: a Jeune/Ellis-van Creveld overlapping presentation. Kidney/liver/retina: unknown here. Survival: unknown. Population: one family in the checked 2015 primary report; ages and follow-up not extracted. Primary reference: E36. Limits: mixed diagnostic features; do not transfer Ellis-van Creveld prognosis to Jeune.

### DYNC2I2 (formerly WDR34)

Association: classification-recognized SRTD/SRPS; established association, phenotype detail preliminary here. Role: motor intermediate chain. Thorax/skeleton: Jeune presentations. Kidney/liver/retina/survival: unknown here. Population: nine families in E22; ages, participant total and longitudinal follow-up not verified from abstract. Primary reference: E22; molecular role E16. Limits: small selected series and possible overlap with other recruitment networks.

### DYNC2I1 (formerly WDR60)

Association: classification-recognized SRTD/SRPS; established association, probable phenotypic range. Role: motor intermediate chain. Thorax/skeleton: severe SRPS and relatively mild Jeune presentations in two families. Kidney/liver/retina/survival: unknown here. Population: original SRPS family plus Jeune family identified among 54 additional skeletal ciliopathy exomes; this is not a Jeune population denominator. Primary reference: E23. Limits: shared variant does not imply shared severity; follow-up not verified.

### DYNLT2B (formerly TCTEX1D2)

Association: classification-recognized SRTD; established association, probable variable expression. Role: motor light chain and stabilization. Thorax/skeleton: five clearly affected people in three families; two additional biallelic siblings lacked diagnostic radiographic proof, with very mild disease not excluded. Kidney/liver/retina: no dependable risk estimate. Survival: respiratory death at two months in E24; E25 adds fetal death and a surviving sibling without the same chest phenotype. Population: E24 screened more than 300 JATD/SRPS cases; E25 two siblings from one family. Primary references: E24, E25. Limits: E24's proposed higher survival and lack of extraskeletal findings are not universal rules; E25 reports cardiac and laterality findings.

## IFT-A genes

### IFT140

Association: classification-recognized SRTD/Mainzer-Saldino; established association, probable phenotype pattern. Role: IFT-A. Thorax/skeleton: often mild chest involvement in a renal-selected study. Kidney: early progressive disease. Retina: findings may appear after initially normal examinations. Liver: unknown here. Survival: no validated rate. Population: E07 mixes JATD and Mainzer-Saldino; four JATD/two MSS biallelic families plus one single-allele JATD family; source wording varies. Primary reference: E07. Limits: do not count all seven as confirmed biallelic JATD or infer mild chest means mild systemic disease.

### WDR19 (IFT144 protein)

Association: classification-recognized SRTD and related ciliopathies; established association, probable renal association. Role: IFT-A. Thorax/skeleton: Jeune, cranioectodermal and isolated renal presentations retain their distinctions. Kidney: nephronophthisis-like disease. Liver/retina: not established for the Jeune subgroup here. Survival: unknown. Population: original report has one family in each of three diagnostic categories. Primary reference: E19. Limits: mixed diagnoses and small samples; no subtype-specific organ frequency.

### TTC21B (IFT139 protein)

Association: classification-recognized SRTD and nephronophthisis; established association. Role: IFT-A. Thorax/skeleton: Jeune and severe SRPS reported. Kidney: isolated or syndromic disease. Liver/retina/survival: unknown here. Population: clinically diverse ciliopathy study; Jeune-specific count, ages and follow-up not verified from abstract. Primary references: E20, E33. Limits: experimental modifying alleles do not establish a general digenic diagnosis or a 5% frequency in Jeune.

### IFT122

Association: classification-listed SRTD/SRPS and cranioectodermal dysplasia. Role: IFT-A. Thorax/skeleton: classification-defined spectrum. Kidney/liver/retina/survival: unknown in this intake. Population: classification rather than an independently extracted Jeune cohort. References: E02, E18 for complex membership. Strength: established classification inclusion; gene-specific Jeune phenotype confidence unknown here. Limits: primary Jeune outcome evidence not independently extracted; no cranioectodermal risk transfer.

### WDR35 (IFT121 protein)

Association: classification-recognized SRTD/SRPS and cranioectodermal dysplasia; established association. Role: IFT-A. Thorax/skeleton: severe SRPS with bowed bones in primary comparison. Kidney/liver/retina/survival: not independently established here. Population: E27 compares IFT43 and WDR35 affected material; exact gene-specific participant count, ages and follow-up not verified. Primary references: E27, E33. Limits: cell findings and severe selected cases do not quantify Jeune prognosis.

### IFT43

Association: classification-recognized SRTD/SRPS and cranioectodermal dysplasia; established association, preliminary source-specific phenotype. Role: IFT-A. Thorax/skeleton: unusual rib and long-bone bending in reported SRPS siblings. Kidney/liver/retina/survival: unknown here. Population: two siblings underpin the illustrated variant record; longitudinal outcome details not independently verified. Primary reference: E27. Limits: perinatal SRPS ascertainment; the ClinVar example has no assertion criteria, not expert-panel review.

## IFT-B genes

### IFT80

Association: classification-recognized Jeune/SRTD and SRPS; established association. Role: IFT-B. Thorax/skeleton: Jeune association in the foundational study. Kidney/liver/retina/survival: no gene-specific human estimate extracted. Population: primary abstract does not supply the screened or affected count, ages or follow-up. Primary reference: E17. Limits: zebrafish kidney findings do not establish human kidney risk; the broader SRPS spectrum is not a uniform Jeune phenotype.

### IFT172

Association: classification-recognized SRTD/Mainzer-Saldino; established association, probable multiorgan pattern. Role: IFT-B. Thorax/skeleton: thoracic and/or long-bone abnormalities. Kidney/liver/retina: involvement reported across the selected affected families, not necessarily every organ in every person. Survival: no validated rate. Population: 12 families identified through renal-ciliopathy screening of 1,467 people and exome analysis of 63 ATD cases; these screening denominators are not affected Jeune counts. Primary reference: E18. Limits: mixed diagnoses, organ-enriched ascertainment, heterogeneous ages/follow-up and possible shared cohorts.

### IFT81

Association: classification-listed SRTD19; ClinGen MODERATE, approved 2025-03-05; UK PanelApp green. Role: IFT-B complex stability and transport. Thorax/skeleton: severe SRPS spectrum. Kidney/liver/retina/survival: unknown for the SRTD subgroup here. Population: primary study and additional literature evaluated by ClinGen; exact primary case total and follow-up not extracted. Primary reference and formal curation: E28. Limits: green inclusion does not convert moderate validity into definitive evidence or predict severity.

### IFT52

Association: classification-recognized SRTD/SRPS; established association, preliminary clinical phenotype detail. Role: IFT-B assembly. Thorax/skeleton: SRPS in a family with two affected fetuses. Kidney/liver/retina: unknown here. Survival: prenatal ascertainment supplies no live-born rate. Population: one family, cellular assays on affected material. Primary reference: E26. Limits: experimental cilia reduction is not a clinical risk percentage.

### TRAF3IP1 (IFT54 protein)

Association: classification-listed SRTD and related renal-retinal ciliopathy; probable specific SRTD association. Role: IFT-B and microtubule regulation. Thorax/skeleton: one ATD and one SRPS case in E33. Kidney/liver/retina: disease reported in a separate renal-retinal series, not established as rates in those two skeletal cases. Survival: unknown. Population: E33's mixed 152-family registry; eight people/five families in its separately cited renal-retinal study. Primary references: E33. Limits: no merging of diagnostic groups; 2023 nosology's IFT154 label differs from the primary IFT54 terminology, retained explicitly as a source discrepancy.

### IFT74: additional primary evidence

Association: probable ATD/SRPS association supported by human and experimental evidence; separate from the 2023 starting list. Role: IFT-B and tubulin transport. Thorax/skeleton: skeletal dysplasia; some alleles also impair airway motile cilia. Kidney: disease reported. Retina: degeneration reported. Liver: no human pattern adopted. Survival: lethal splice-variant presentations and surviving deletion cases. Population: five people/four families, with differing alleles and follow-up. Primary reference: E34; E10 is a later severe case, not pooled. Limits: no universal allele-to-course rule; study-level genotype interpretation is not independent clinical variant review.

## Cilia assembly and signaling genes

### CEP120

Association: classification-recognized SRTD and separate Joubert spectrum; established association. Role: centriole formation. Thorax/skeleton: severe Jeune/SRPS-like disease. Kidney/liver/retina/survival: no gene-specific human estimate extracted. Population: four families sharing the founder p.Ala199Pro variant; participant total, ages and follow-up not verified from abstract. Primary reference: E21. Limits: four families with a shared ancestral allele are not four independent allelic replications; zebrafish renal findings are not human rates.

### CFAP410 (formerly C21orf2)

Association: classification-recognized SRTD/axial skeletal disease and retinal disease; established association, probable variable expression. Role: ciliary protein; specific function incompletely resolved. Thorax/skeleton: syndromic cases exist, but the selected newer study is retinal-predominant. Kidney/liver/survival: unknown here. Retina: early-onset degeneration. Population: 16 families in the final peer-reviewed retinal study, not a Jeune cohort. Primary reference: E29. Limits: no clear severity correlation found; its earlier preprint is the same study, not independent replication.

### KIAA0586 (TALPID3 protein)

Association: classification-recognized SRTD and Joubert-related spectrum; established association. Role: centrosomal protein needed for primary cilia formation. Thorax/skeleton: lethal ciliopathies ranging from hydrolethalus to SRPS. Kidney/liver/retina: not established here for a Jeune subgroup. Survival: severe selected prenatal/lethal presentations, not universal lethality. Population: four families, mixed diagnoses; longitudinal ages not verified. Primary reference: E30. Limits: severe alleles and selected diagnoses cannot define all KIAA0586 outcomes.

### KIAA0753

Association: classification-recognized SRTD21/Joubert/orofaciodigital spectrum; established association, probable variability. Role: centrosomal component. Thorax/skeleton: narrow chest and metaphyseal abnormalities with varying severity. Kidney/liver/retina: unknown here. Survival: one fetal lethal presentation and three viable children in the 2017 series. Population: four people, with Joubert features in some; longer-term follow-up not extracted. Primary reference: E31. Limits: small mixed spectrum; no deterministic prediction from variant class.

### GRK2 (ADRBK1 alias)

Association: classification-listed Jeune/SRTD; probable clinical association, UK skeletal ciliopathy panel amber. Role: Hedgehog and Wnt signaling regulation; cilia can remain morphologically intact. Thorax/skeleton: narrow chest, short/bowed bones. Kidney/liver/retina: unknown here. Survival: three people/two families in 2020; deaths at five days and 5.5 months, sibling alive at 12 months. Population: small discovery series, plus one independently reported girl in E32's later report. Primary references: E32. Limits: later case supports association but does not establish a validated severity model; no formal ClinGen grade is claimed here.

### FGF4: newer thoracic dystrophy report

Association: preliminary human thoracic dystrophy association; UK skeletal dysplasia panel green, Australian Mendeliome activity amber. Role: growth-factor signaling; specific reported variant effects and human ciliary mechanism unconfirmed. Thorax/skeleton: short or missing ribs and narrow chest without typical limb shortening or trident pelvis. Kidney/liver/retina: no risk estimate. Survival: one person assessed at 20 years, one death at three months, one assessed at two years with ongoing respiratory insufficiency. Population: three people/two unrelated families. Primary reference: E35. Limits: distinct phenotype and missing functional confirmation; do not redefine all Jeune as FGF4 disease or infer normal lifetime extra-thoracic organs.

## Related spectrum boundary

The classification separately lists NEK1 and INTU under SRPS, CILK1 under endocrine-cerebro-osteo dysplasia, and CSPP1 under Joubert syndrome with SRTD. These are relevant differential/spectrum associations, not automatically classic Jeune cohorts. Detailed kidney, liver, retina, survival and participant data for these related diagnoses were not extracted here. Confidence: established classification placement, unknown Jeune-specific prognosis. [E02, E15]

E33 also reports FUZ and LBR associations within a mixed skeletal ciliopathy cohort. These remain preliminary/contextual for this map, with no gene-specific organ or survival conclusion adopted. They are retained to prevent the starting classification list from being mistaken for exhaustive coverage. Other phenotype panels may include additional ciliary genes; membership alone is not a completed gene-disease assessment. [E33]

## Important variant examples and interpretation limits

- IFT43 NM_001102564.3:c.2T>A, p.Met1Lys: reported in SRPS siblings with segregation and functional context. The checked condition-specific ClinVar record RCV000851214.1 lists likely pathogenic, one submission, no assertion criteria, last evaluated 2017-03-30. This is a dated submitter assertion, not expert-panel consensus. [E27, E28 supplemental ClinVar record]
- IFT74 exon-2 deletions and splice-region variants: distinct skeletal/airway presentations and experimental effects. Structural and transcript effects matter; variant class alone does not predict course. Mouse null lethality is not a human penetrance estimate. [E34]
- FGF4 NM_002007.4:c.256C>T, p.Leu86Phe and c.611C>A, p.Pro204His: reported homozygous in the two families, with parental segregation. Authors report population absence and damaging computational predictions, but request variant-specific functional validation. Computational labels such as "likely pathogenic" in prediction software are not a clinical laboratory classification. No current ClinVar consensus was independently established for these variants here. [E35]

These are illustrative, not a comprehensive pathogenic-variant catalog. No clinical classification is reassigned. An uncertain variant remains uncertain without sufficient independent evidence, even in a well-established disease gene.
